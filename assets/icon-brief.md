Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool from the existing `icon.png`, then resized with `sips` for the extension manifest. The high-resolution output is `assets/icon-master.png`; the shipping icon is `icon.png` at 256 × 256.

Final prompt: Preserve the exact existing two stacked flowing wave silhouettes, their positions, proportions, negative space, and overall composition. Recreate the edges at high resolution so they remain crisp at extension-icon sizes. Use a solid dark navy #111827 backdrop, pale cyan #A5F3FC for the upper wave, and blue #60A5FA for the lower wave, drawn from Darkwind's palette. Keep a flat, vector-clean graphic with sharp tips and clear separation. No lettering or additional elements.

The palette artwork has a separate editable source in `assets/palette.svg`. Only the rasterized PNG is used in the README and VSIX.
